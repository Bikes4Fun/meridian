What is Meridian?

A home system for people living with cognitive decline and the families who support them.

Meridian is built around a kiosk — a large-format touchscreen meant to stay in one place, stay visible, and stay on. The person at home uses it to check the time and schedule, see where family members are, read messages, manage medications, and reach emergency information. No login required.

Family members use the web dashboard to manage shared data: events, medications, and the emergency/ICE profile. The mobile app (iOS, in development) handles location check-ins and responses to "Where is everyone?" requests.

⚠️
Early accessAuthentication and encryption are not yet production-hardened. Use test or demo data only unless your administrator has confirmed the deployment is ready.

Overview

Family Circles

All data in Meridian belongs to a Family Circle — visible only to people you've invited.

Each circle has one care recipient. Everyone else joins with a role controlling what they can see and change. The kiosk is pre-authenticated to the circle — the care recipient never signs in.

Kiosk

Home screen

The default screen. Shows the time, date, upcoming events, and today's medications.

  • Clock — large-format current time
  • Date and time of day — morning / afternoon / evening, for orientation
  • Up Next — the next upcoming calendar event
  • Today's medications — doses for the current time window, each with a Mark taken button

Marking a dose taken refreshes the screen automatically.

Kiosk

Schedule screen

A full view of upcoming events from the shared family calendar.

Events are listed in chronological order. Managed from the web dashboard — changes appear on the kiosk the next time the screen loads.

Kiosk

Family screen

Shows where family members are. Includes the "Where is everyone?" button.

Displays a map with each family member's most recent check-in. If the check-in falls within a named location, the kiosk shows the label (e.g. "Anne is at work downtown") instead of a pin.

Tapping Where is everyone? sends a check-in request to all family members' phones.

💡
The kiosk's own location can be named — so the care recipient sees "You are at your home on Maple Street" rather than a pin at their address.
Kiosk

Chat screen

Send messages and start video calls with family members.

Lists family members available to contact. Tapping a name opens a conversation. Video calls can also be started from this screen. Family members join from the web dashboard or mobile app.

Kiosk

Health screen

Full medication schedule with Mark taken buttons.

Medications are grouped by time of day (morning, afternoon, evening) and PRN (as needed). The list is managed from the web dashboard — changes sync immediately.

💡
Doses can also be marked taken from the Home screen during the relevant time window, without navigating here.
Kiosk

Emergency screen

The care recipient's full emergency profile. Accessible any time via the red Emergency button in the footer.

Displays everything from the ICE profile maintained in the web dashboard:

  • Name, date of birth, DNR / code status
  • Conditions and allergies
  • Current medications
  • Emergency contacts (primary and secondary)
  • Medical proxy and power of attorney (name and phone)
  • Notes
  • Open document — if a POLST or DNR file has been uploaded

A Print button generates a formatted emergency PDF. The kiosk may auto-print when an alert is active, depending on configuration.

⚠️
Keep it currentReview medications and code status after any medical appointment or change in directives.

Emergency alert mode

Activating an emergency alert from the web dashboard (Settings → Kiosk & TV) switches the kiosk to this screen. Canceling it from the dashboard returns the kiosk to normal. Use only in a real emergency.

Medications can be added, edited, or removed from the same list that appears on the Health screen and in the emergency profile.

Main entry point for health data — POLST profile, medications, and the kiosk emergency alert.

  • POLST & emergency (ICE) — link to the ICE profile editor
  • Kiosk emergency alert — activate or cancel the alert on the kiosk
  • Medications — inline add/edit/remove, and mark doses taken

Managing medications

The same list that appears on the kiosk Health screen and in the emergency profile. Changes sync immediately.

To remove medications: check the box next to each one, then choose Remove selected. Select all selects every row; clicking again clears the selection.

Medical proxy & POA

Name and phone for both the medical proxy and power of attorney. Both appear on the kiosk Emergency screen.

Emergency contacts

Each contact has a name, phone, relationship, and priority: Primary emergency, Secondary emergency, or Not on ICE list. Only primary and secondary contacts appear on the Emergency screen. Use Add emergency contact to add a row; remove with the Remove button. Saved with Save profile.

Conditions, allergies & medications

Conditions and allergies are read-only (pulled from the medical record). The medication list is editable inline. Save with Save profile.

Photo & DNR document

Two separate uploads, each with its own upload button. Both save immediately on file selection — no need to hit Save profile.

  • Care recipient photo — shown on the kiosk Emergency screen
  • DNR or POLST file (PDF, image, DOC, DOCX) — after upload, a View uploaded document link appears here and an Open document button appears on the kiosk Emergency screen
💡
SavingUse Save profile for name, DOB, DNR status, notes, proxy/POA, contacts, and medications together. Photo and document uploads save on their own immediately.

Print emergency document

Print Emergency Document opens a formatted PDF summary in a new tab. Use your browser's print dialog to print or save as PDF.

Mobile App

Check-in & location

How family members share their location so it appears on the kiosk Family screen.

A check-in submits the family member's current location, which is matched against any named locations set up for the circle. Check-ins can include an optional notes field that appears alongside the location on the kiosk.

🕐
iOS app in developmentThe check-in flow is currently available via the web dashboard's mobile-optimized view.
Mobile App

Where is everyone?

Tapping this on the kiosk Family screen sends a push notification to family members asking them to check in.

  1. The kiosk user taps Where is everyone? on the Family screen.
  2. A push notification goes out to all participating family members.
  3. The next time they open the app, they can accept or decline. In progress
  4. If they accept, their location is submitted. They can also add a short note. Video messages planned
  5. Their location appears on the kiosk Family screen.

Rules & limits

  • Requests expire after 24 hours if unanswered Coming
  • Only one active request per family member at a time Coming
  • Max request frequency is configurable Coming
  • Location sharing is always opt-in — family members can decline any request
💡
Push notifications require APNs configuration on the server. Without it, requests are logged but not delivered. Contact your administrator if notifications aren't arriving.
Reference

Named locations

Give common places a plain-language label. When a check-in falls within the location's radius, the kiosk shows the label instead of a map pin.

Location matching uses a haversine distance calculation. The nearest matching location wins when areas overlap. The default radius is 150 meters; each location's radius is configurable.

🕐
Editor in progressServer-side matching is live. Once the editor ships, locations you define apply to new check-ins immediately.

How families write labels

Labels can be brief or descriptive — the goal is that whoever reads it, including someone who may be disoriented, immediately understands where the person is and that it's somewhere expected.

  • "Anne is at work downtown"
  • "Evan is at High School in Colorado Springs"
  • "Dave's Office"
  • "You are at your home on Maple Street in Denver" — for the kiosk's own location

Data & privacy

What Meridian stores, who can see it, and what's protected.

Circle isolation

All data is scoped to your Family Circle. No other users or circles can access it. The kiosk is pre-authenticated — no credentials are ever exposed to the care recipient.

Location data

Location is submitted only when a family member actively responds to a check-in. No passive background tracking. Location history is not stored by default.

Health & medical data

Medications, conditions, allergies, and ICE documents are stored within your circle only. Uploaded POLST/DNR documents can be deleted at any time from the ICE editor.

Transport

All data is transmitted over TLS. Uploaded documents use access-controlled storage links.

⚠️
Not yet production-hardenedFull encryption at rest, authentication hardening, and compliance certifications are in progress. Do not store real private or medical data unless your administrator has confirmed the deployment is ready.
