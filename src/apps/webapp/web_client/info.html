Overview

What is Meridian?

Meridian connects families, caregivers, and emergency responders around a single, always-current source of truth for the person they're caring for.

Day-to-day care involves a lot of people holding a lot of partial information — one sibling knows about a medication change, another remembers the appointment, no one can find the POLST form when the paramedics ask. Meridian brings it all into one shared system, accessible from the web, your phone, or a dedicated kiosk that the person receiving care can use without needing to manage any technology themselves.

Behavior and available features can vary slightly by deployment — home use, facility use, test environments, and device type may each look a little different. This guide describes how the platform works in general terms; your specific setup may differ.

Core Concept

Family Circles

A Family Circle is the shared space where all of your care information lives — events, medications, locations, contacts, and emergency documents — visible only to the people you invite.

Every household or care unit in Meridian is organized as a Family Circle. Think of it as a private, shared environment for one care situation. People outside your circle cannot see anything inside it.

Each circle has one designated care recipient — the person being supported. Health data, emergency documents, and POLST directives are associated with that person. Everyone else in the circle is a family member, caregiver, or administrator, each with a role that controls what they can view and change.

About roles & permissions

Different actions in Meridian — editing medications, managing location settings, updating billing — require different permission levels. If you're unsure what you can change in your deployment, check with whoever set up your circle or your facility administrator.

Feature

Named Locations

Instead of showing a map pin or a street address, Meridian shows whoever is at the kiosk a message they can actually understand — and trust.

Family members with editor permissions can name the places their loved one's family regularly goes. Each location gets a natural-language label and a configurable geofence radius. The labels can be as simple or as detailed as your family finds helpful.

  • 📍 "Anne is at work downtown"
  • 📍 "Evan is at High School in Colorado Springs"
  • 📍 "Dave's Office"
  • 📍 "You are at your home on Maple Street in Denver"

The kiosk's own location can also be named — so the care recipient can see that they are at home, described in familiar terms, rather than looking at an address or a map they may not recognize.

Why this matters

For someone experiencing cognitive decline, a map showing a dot labeled "downtown" does little to ease anxiety about where a family member has gone. A message that says "Anne is at work — she'll be home this evening" speaks in the language of relationships. It provides not just information but reassurance.

Paranoia and distrust of unfamiliar technology are also common symptoms for people living with dementia. Being able to request that family confirm the information is current — rather than simply reading it off a screen — can make a real difference in whether a concerned person feels settled or continues to escalate.

Tip — naming the kiosk location

If the kiosk is in the care recipient's home, set a named location for that address with a warm, recognizable label. Seeing "You are at your home on Maple Street" can be grounding in a way that an address or map coordinates simply aren't.

Feature

Where Is Everyone?

A single tap on the kiosk sends a check-in request to every family member's phone — and their responses come back to the kiosk as messages or videos.

When the kiosk user initiates Where Is Everyone?, participating family members receive a push notification on their mobile app. Tapping the notification requests a current location update and, optionally, a brief check-in message or short video — something personal, not just a location ping.

The system is designed around consent. Family members choose whether to respond each time. No one's location is shared silently or without their knowledge.

How it works

StepWhat happens

Feature

Emergency & POLST

Meridian gives your family one place to keep life-critical emergency information current — and puts it in front of first responders instantly when it's needed.

The In Case of Emergency (ICE) profile is where your family maintains the care recipient's complete emergency record. You update it from the web dashboard; it's always accessible on the kiosk's Emergency screen and printable on demand.

Managing the ICE profile (web dashboard)

Open POLST & Emergency (ICE) from the main dashboard navigation. From there you can:

🪪

Identity & Code Status

Name, date of birth, DNR / full-code status, and care directives in plain language.

📋

Medical Proxy & POA

Designee names stored alongside the profile they govern — no separate document to dig up.

💊

Medications & Contacts

The same list shared across the circle. Select all / deselect all for bulk updates. Always current.

📎

POLST / DNR Upload

Upload a scanned order (PDF or image). Immediately accessible from the kiosk and via a direct link in the editor.

🖼️

Care Recipient Photo

Upload a photo — used in the editor preview and on the kiosk when available.

🖨️

Print Emergency Document

Generate a print-ready PDF summary in one click — for paramedics, ER staff, or your own records.

Saving your changes

Use Save profile to persist text fields, medications, and contact updates together. Photo and POLST/DNR uploads save immediately when you choose a file — you don't need to hit Save for those.

On the kiosk — Emergency screen

The kiosk's Emergency screen displays the full composed profile: name, date of birth, code status, allergies, medications, conditions, contacts, proxy, and POA. If a POLST or DNR document has been uploaded, an Open document button gives immediate access to the scanned orders — no login needed from a first responder's perspective.

Depending on your deployment configuration, the kiosk may also offer Print Emergency Document directly, and may auto-print when an emergency alert is active.

Keep it current

The emergency profile is only as useful as it is accurate. We recommend reviewing medications, contacts, and code status any time there's a change — and after every medical appointment where directives may have been updated.

Emergency alerts

Broader alert behavior — notifications to family members, escalation chains, and how alerts interact with kiosk navigation and printing — is still evolving. Your deployment's specific configuration is the source of truth for how alerts behave in your setup.

Safety & Security

How Your Data Is Protected

Meridian handles some of the most sensitive information a family holds. Security is built into every layer of the platform — not added on afterward.

  • 01
    Encrypted in transitAll data between your devices and Meridian's servers travels over TLS encryption. Nothing sensitive is ever sent in plaintext.
  • 02
    Role-based access controlEvery action — viewing medications, editing locations, managing billing, accessing emergency documents — is independently gated by role. Family members can only see and change what their role permits.
  • 03
    Circle isolationYour Family Circle's data is strictly isolated. Even in a shared deployment, no data can cross between circles.
  • 04
    Consent-based location sharingLocation data is shared only when a family member actively responds to a check-in request. Meridian does not passively track or store location in the background.
  • 05
    Locked-down kioskThe kiosk runs as a single-purpose device. It does not expose a general browser, filesystem, or operating system to the user. In facility deployments, sensitive screens can be configured to require a staff PIN.
  • 06
    Secure document storageUploaded POLST and DNR files are stored with access-controlled links. The underlying file storage path is never exposed. Emergency access on the kiosk uses time-limited or session-scoped tokens.
  • 07
    Audit loggingSignificant actions — profile edits, document uploads, emergency screen access — are logged with user identity, timestamp, and context for review if needed.
  • 08
    Session managementWeb dashboard sessions time out after inactivity. Mobile apps use platform-native biometric or PIN re-authentication. Kiosk sessions are pre-authenticated per deployment — the person receiving care never needs to manage a password.
Privacy

Your Information, Your Control

Meridian collects what it needs to deliver each feature — nothing more. You can see, update, and delete your family's information at any time.

Type of dataHow it's handled
Location dataShared only when a family member actively responds to a check-in. Not stored as a continuous history unless your family opts into a location history feature.
Health & medical informationScoped strictly to your Family Circle. Not used for advertising or shared with third parties outside your deployment's data agreements.
POLST / DNR documentsStored with encrypted, access-controlled links. You can delete an uploaded document at any time from the ICE editor.
Family member accountsEach person signs in with their own account. Account data is not shared across circles.
Minors in the circleIf your circle includes minors (grandchildren, for example), their location data is subject to additional consent and parental control requirements.
Regulatory context

Meridian's architecture is designed for alignment with HIPAA in healthcare deployments and GDPR / CCPA in consumer contexts. Specific compliance attestations depend on your deployment configuration. If you have questions about data handling in a facility deployment, ask your administrator.

🔡

Large-Format Kiosk UI

Typography and touch targets are sized for low-vision users and people with reduced fine motor control.

🗣️

Plain Language

Every system message, label, and confirmation is written for clarity. Technical jargon is avoided throughout.

🧭

No-Login Kiosk

The care recipient never manages a password. The kiosk is always on and always ready.

🤝

Dementia-Informed Design

Named locations, familiar language, and calming information display reflect best practices for people with cognitive challenges.

🌐

Localization Support

The platform is built for multi-language deployment. Families can operate in the language that's most natural for the care recipient.

♿

The web dashboard and mobile apps target WCAG 2.1 AA, with screen-reader support and keyboard navigability.

Deployment

Meridian works for families managing care at home and for operators running assisted living, memory care, or home care programs.

Optional — stove / kitchen temperature (kiosk)

Some setups attach a USB temperature probe to the kiosk. The current reading appears under Settings → Monitors on the kiosk. Serial port and alert behavior are configured on the machine that runs the kiosk (for example STOVE_SERIAL_PORT in the project .env), not in this web dashboard.

Test vs. production

Meridian supports separate test and production environments. Feature availability, notification routing, and emergency alert behavior can differ between them. If something behaves unexpectedly, confirm with your administrator whether you're in a test environment.
