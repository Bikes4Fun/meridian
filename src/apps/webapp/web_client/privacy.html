Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

Meridian collects contact and message information needed to deliver family care messaging and call notifications.

Information we collect

  • Phone numbers used for Meridian messaging and voice notifications
  • Message content and delivery metadata (sent time and status)
  • Basic account identifiers needed to deliver the service

How we use information

  • Deliver family care updates and emergency communication
  • Provide support and troubleshoot delivery issues
  • Maintain reliability, security, and compliance logs

Data sharing

Meridian does not sell personal information. Meridian does not share mobile information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your controls

You can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP.

Contact

For support, reply HELP to a Meridian message or contact your Meridian administrator.
