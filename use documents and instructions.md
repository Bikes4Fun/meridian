# User Guide

## Named Locations
Family members with family settings editor permissions (TODO: name this permissions level?) can name the locations that their family frequents. These can be as verbose or minimal as the family desires.

Examples: An adult daughter may add her office as "Anne is at work downtown," a grandchild's school may be defined as "Evan is at High School in Colorado Springs," or simply "Dave's Office."

Family editors can set radius ranges for these locations.

Since kiosk users may forget, not be familiar with the location, or may find maps confusing and disorienting, naming these common family locations is intended to help a a kiosk user who is experiencing distress about a loved one's whereabouts and safety to confirm that the person is in a location they are "supposed" to be in.

Additionally, paranoia is a common symptom (citation) and as such, so may be being distrustful of even clearly displayed information, particularly if the method of delivery or technology is not familiar. Simply walking up and seeing a map or list of family locations may not always provide a patient with the reassurance they need. Being able to clearly request that their family confirms the information as current, timely, and accurate may help relieve potential distrust and paranoia about the accuracy and reliability of technology.

Finally, the kiosk location itself can be named, allowing families to set the patient's own home as a named location and tag the patient as being "at home." An example might be: "You are at your home on Maple Street in Denver."

# Where Is Everyone?
When a kiosk user requests **Where is everyone?**, mobile app users receive a push notification. Tapping the notification requests a location update and submits a check-in.

The next time they open the app, they will be prompted to accept or decline.\\* If they accept, they will also be prompted to accept or decline sending a brief check-in chat message or video message.\\* Check-in requests expire 24 hours after they are sent. If the app user does not follow the push or open the app for more than 24 hours, they will no longer see the notification.\\* While a check-in request is active, no further check-in requests will be sent to that family member.\\* A maximum check-in request frequency should be set.
